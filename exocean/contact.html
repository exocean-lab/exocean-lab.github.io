Join us

- Students, early-career researchers and visiting scientists are welcome all year round.

Internships

We host Master's and undergraduate interns every year, usually for two to six months, on topics ranging from foraminifera culture and microscopy to carbonate chemistry, high-pressure experiments and sediment reactors. Write to us with a CV and a few lines on what you would like to learn — we can usually shape a project together.

PhD and postdoctoral positions

Funded positions are announced on our Bluesky account as they open. We are also glad to hear from candidates who would like to prepare a fellowship application with us (Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions and similar schemes). Send a CV, a short statement of interests and the name of the team member whose work is closest to yours.

Visiting scientists and facility access

Colleagues who would like to run experiments with live planktonic foraminifera at COREFF, use our 500-bar reactor or the rotating disk reactors, or spend time in the laboratory can contact Julie MEILLAND (cultures) or Olivier SULPIS (reactors) directly. Analyses we can run for you are listed on our Services page.

Write to us at — a short e-mail is all it takes.
