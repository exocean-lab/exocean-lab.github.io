Models

RADI

Reaction-transport model of early diagenesis in marine sediments

The reactive-advective-diffusive-irrigative model simulates, through time, the chemistry of surface sediments and their exchanges with the overlying seawater, in Julia (Radi.jl) and MATLAB/GNU Octave (Radi.m). RADIv2 handles coastal and open-ocean sediments alike.

From the team: Olivier SULPIS

Datasets

FORCIS

Global census of living planktonic foraminifera

Distribution and diversity of planktonic foraminifera from a century of plankton-net, pump, sediment-trap and continuous-plankton-recorder samples across the world ocean, with an R package to download, filter, harmonise and map the data.

From the team: Sonia CHAABANE and Julie MEILLAND

Cenozoic CO₂ (CenCO2PIP)

66 million years of atmospheric CO₂ from proxy records

A community-vetted synthesis of atmospheric CO₂ over the Cenozoic, assembled by the Cenozoic CO₂ Proxy Integration Project consortium from marine and terrestrial proxy records. Every record and its evaluation is openly archived.

From the team: Thomas CHALK

Micro-CT images of planktonic foraminifera

3D scans of shells with curated taxonomy

X-ray micro-computed tomography images of 179 shells covering 43 of the 48 recognised species and subspecies of holoplanktonic foraminifera, with their metadata.

From the team: Julie MEILLAND
