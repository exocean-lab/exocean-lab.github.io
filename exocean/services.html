Services & Instruments

- Analyses and experiments we run for partners, and the equipment behind them.

Beyond our own research, we run measurements and experiments for academic and private partners — within a collaboration, or as a paid service. You can send us your samples, or come and work with us in the laboratory.

On this page: What we offer · Our instruments

How to request an analysis

Write to us at with a few lines on your samples (type, number, volume) and on what you need. We will tell you what is feasible and how long it will take, and send a quote for paid services.

What we offer

Seawater carbonate chemistry

Total alkalinity by potentiometric titration, on sample volumes down to 4 mL, and pH by spectrophotometry with purified indicator dye. Alkalinity measurements are calibrated with Dickson certified reference materials.

Instruments: Metrohm OMNIS titrator · Agilent Cary 60 spectrophotometer

Dissolved silicate and phosphate

Concentrations of the nutrients silicate and phosphate in seawater, measured by colorimetry on a UV-Vis spectrophotometer.

Instruments: Agilent Cary 60 spectrophotometer

Microsensor profiling

Microprofiles of oxygen, pH, nitric oxide (NO) and hydrogen sulfide through sediments and other confined environments. The setup is mobile: profiles can be measured in the lab, on ships right after sediment collection, or at coastal sites.

Instruments: Unisense microsensor profiling system

Respiration of single organisms

Respiration of microbes, single foraminifera or other small plankton, measured with microsensors in very small volumes of water under a stereomicroscope.

Instruments: Unisense NanoRespiration system · Zeiss Stemi 508 stereomicroscope

Sediment–water interface experiments

Sediment incubations in six temperature-controlled reactors that reproduce benthic hydrodynamics, from calm abyssal plains to high-energy settings such as reefs. Open or closed to the atmosphere, in free drift or flow-through, with chemistry followed in the porewaters and in the overlying water.

Instruments: Rotating disk reactors (×6) · Julabo CORIO refrigerated circulator

High-pressure incubations

Water or solid samples held at up to 500 bar (roughly the pressure at 5,000 m depth) in a 600 mL vessel, with water sampling during the run. Gas composition cannot be measured with the current setup.

Instruments: Büchiglas Novoclave high-pressure reactor

Cultures and incubations

Culture of plankton, microbes and other small organisms, and incubation experiments, under controlled temperature and light — from 0 to above 35 °C in the incubators of our COREFF foraminifera facility. Light intensity and spectrum are measured with a PAR sensor.

Instruments: Friocell 111 EVO and Memmert incubators · PAR sensor

Microscopy and imaging

Observation and imaging of living plankton and other immersed samples on an inverted microscope, and of small samples under a stereomicroscope.

Instruments: Zeiss Axiovert 5 inverted microscope · Zeiss Stemi 508 stereomicroscope

Plasma cleaning of samples

Removal of organic matter from samples by plasma cleaning.

Instruments: Henniker HPT-100 plasma cleaner

Our instruments

The equipment of the exocean laboratory at CEREGE, by type.

Chemical analyses

Metrohm OMNIS titrator
Total alkalinity of seawater by potentiometric titration, on small volumes.
Agilent Cary 60 UV-Vis spectrophotometer
Seawater chemistry by spectrophotometry: pH with purified indicator dye, dissolved silicate and phosphate.
Unisense microsensor profiling system
Microsensors for oxygen, pH, nitric oxide (NO) and hydrogen sulfide on a motorized, automated vertical micromanipulator.

Experimental systems

Büchiglas Novoclave high-pressure reactor
600 mL vessel for water or solid samples, up to 500 bar.
Rotating disk reactors (×6)
Stirred or rotating-disk reactors that reproduce benthic hydrodynamics, with chemical monitoring.
Unisense NanoRespiration system
Incubation of microbes or plankton in very small volumes of water, followed with microsensors.
Friocell 111 EVO (×2) and Memmert incubators
Culture of microbes, plankton and other small organisms, and incubations, under controlled temperature and light.

Microscopy

Zeiss Axiovert 5 inverted microscope
Observation and imaging of living plankton and other immersed samples.
Zeiss Stemi 508 stereomicroscope
Observation of samples and of microsensor tips.
