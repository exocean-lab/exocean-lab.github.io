Join us

Students, early-career researchers and visiting scientists are welcome all year round.

Open positions

There is no open position right now. Funded positions are announced on this page and on our Bluesky account as they open — and we are glad to help you apply for a fellowship to work with us (see below).

Internships

We host Master's and undergraduate interns every year, usually for two to six months, on topics ranging from foraminifera culture and microscopy to carbonate chemistry, high-pressure experiments and sediment reactors. Write to us at with a CV and a few lines on what you would like to learn — we can usually shape a project together.

PhD and postdoctoral positions

We recruit PhD students and postdocs on our funded projects; open positions are listed above. To introduce yourself, send a CV, a short statement of interests and the name of the team member whose work is closest to yours — the People page says what everyone works on.

Fellowships

We are glad to hear from candidates who would like to prepare a fellowship application with us, such as a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellowship. Two of our postdocs, Astrid Hylén and Jaime Suárez-Ibarra, hold Marie Skłodowska-Curie fellowships. Get in touch several months before the call deadline: a good application takes time.

Visiting scientists and facility access

Colleagues who would like to run experiments with live planktonic foraminifera at COREFF, use our 500-bar reactor or the rotating disk reactors, or spend time in the laboratory can contact Julie Meilland (cultures) or Olivier Sulpis (reactors) directly; visitors pay a small lab occupation fee. Analyses we can run for you are listed on our Services page.

How to apply

Write to with a CV and a few lines on what you would like to do. For an internship, say when and for how long.

Working with us

Our researchers belong to CEREGE's Climate team, and the laboratory itself is one of CEREGE's technical platforms. CEREGE is on the Arbois plateau near Aix-en-Provence, with Marseille and the Mediterranean close by.
