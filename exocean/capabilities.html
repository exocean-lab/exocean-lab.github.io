Analyses and experiments for partners

Experimental Oceanology Laboratory · CEREGE, Aix-en-Provence, France

Beyond our own research, we run measurements and experiments for academic and private partners — within a collaboration, or as a paid service. You can send us your samples, or come and work with us in the laboratory; visitors pay a small lab occupation fee.

Water and sediment chemistry

Seawater carbonate chemistry. Total alkalinity by potentiometric titration on samples down to 4 mL, and pH by spectrophotometry with purified indicator dye. Alkalinity is calibrated with Dickson certified reference materials.

Dissolved silicate and phosphate. Silicate and phosphate concentrations in seawater, by colorimetry on a UV-Vis spectrophotometer.

Microsensor profiling. Microprofiles of oxygen, pH, nitric oxide (NO) and hydrogen sulfide through sediments and other confined environments — in the lab, on ships right after sediment collection, or at coastal sites.

Experiments and incubations

Sediment–water interface experiments. Sediment incubations in six temperature-controlled reactors that reproduce benthic hydrodynamics, from calm abyssal plains to reefs. Open or closed to the atmosphere, in free drift or flow-through, with chemistry followed in the porewaters and the overlying water.

High-pressure incubations. Water or solid samples held at up to 500 bar (roughly the pressure at 5,000 m depth) in a 600 mL vessel, with water sampling during the run. Gas composition cannot be measured with the current setup.

Cultures and incubations. Culture of plankton, microbes and other small organisms, and incubation experiments, under controlled temperature and light — from 0 to above 35 °C in the incubators of our COREFF foraminifera facility. Light intensity and spectrum are measured with a PAR sensor.

Organisms and samples

Respiration of single organisms. Respiration of microbes, single foraminifera or other small plankton, measured with microsensors in very small volumes of water under a stereomicroscope.

Microscopy and imaging. Observation and imaging of living plankton and other immersed samples on an inverted microscope, and of small samples under a stereomicroscope.

Plasma cleaning of samples. Removal of organic matter from samples by plasma cleaning.

Instruments

Metrohm OMNIS titrator · Agilent Cary 60 UV-Vis spectrophotometer · Unisense microsensor profiling system · Büchiglas Novoclave high-pressure reactor · Rotating disk reactors (×6) · Unisense NanoRespiration system · Friocell 111 EVO (×2) and Memmert incubators · Zeiss Axiovert 5 inverted microscope · Zeiss Stemi 508 stereomicroscope · PAR sensor · Julabo CORIO refrigerated circulator · Henniker HPT-100 plasma cleaner · −80 °C freezer · Laboratory refrigerator

Other CEREGE platforms

A visit can be combined with PANISS (stable isotopes), LA-ICP-MS (elemental chemistry), LGO2i (radiocarbon, biomarkers and sediment geochemistry), MATRIX (2D and 3D X-ray imaging), MANTA (automated micropalaeontology), LPM (microfossil extraction), SEDIM (sedimentology).

How to ask: write to exocean@cerege.fr with a few lines on your samples (type, number, volume) and what you need. We reply with what is feasible, how to prepare your samples and how long it will take — with a quote for paid services. Visitors pay a small lab occupation fee.
