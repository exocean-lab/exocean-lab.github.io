Ezra FINNEY

Master's Student at Aix-Marseille Université, France

Master's intern from January 2027

Salut !

I am a Master's student in Sciences de la mer at Aix-Marseille Université (Institut Pythéas), on the biology and ecology track, and I will join the exocean laboratory in January 2027 for my Master's research internship, on microbial incubations in the high-pressure reactors.

I first worked with the team in 2025, during my Licence, on a data-mining project combining seafloor respiration measurements, carbonate content and bathymetry to see how sediment chemistry is organised in the deepest ocean trenches — whether trenches act as funnels for carbon. I am coming back to continue on the experimental side.
