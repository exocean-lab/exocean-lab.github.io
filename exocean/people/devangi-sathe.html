Devangi Sathe

CNRS postdoctoral researcher

Carbonate solubility at abyssal and hadal pressures

Hello!

I am a postdoctoral researcher on the ERC project Deep-C at CEREGE, where I study how calcium carbonate dissolves in the deep sea. Using the laboratory's high-pressure reactors, I measure the solubility of different carbonate minerals at the pressures of the abyssal and hadal seafloor, and how fast they dissolve when microbes are respiring around them.

Before coming to Aix-en-Provence I did my PhD with Ronnie Glud at the University of Southern Denmark (HADAL & Nordcee, Odense), studying microbial life under high pressure. That background in experimental microbiology is what brought me to the reactors: my aim is to understand how respiration in sediments drives carbonate dissolution, from incubations of coastal sediment to experiments at 1,000 bar.
