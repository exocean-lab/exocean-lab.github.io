Maxime FELIX

CNRS Marine Biogeochemistry Research Engineer at CEREGE, France

Bonjour !

I am a research engineer in the Climate team at CEREGE. My work focuses on management of hyperbaric equipment and measurement of carbonate chemistry at the Exocean laboratory. Passionate about ocean dynamics, my aim is to advance scientific research in deep sea waters, but also to enable the successful development of various projects within the laboratory.

Before joining CEREGE, I studied marine science with a specialization in anthropogenic disturbances at the University of Toulon, where I developed an interest in research as well as in laboratory instruments and analytical techniques.

Outside the lab, I enjoy sports such as tennis, running, and football, and I also appreciate discovering marine wildlife while freediving.
