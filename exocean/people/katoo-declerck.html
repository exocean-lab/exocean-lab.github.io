Katoo Declerck

PhD student

Dissolution of single foraminifera shells

Hallo!

I am a biologist and PhD student at the exocean laboratory in CEREGE, supervised by Olivier Sulpis and Julie Meilland. My thesis follows deep-sea calcium carbonate dissolution from the microenvironment of a single foraminifera shell to the global carbon cycle, combining micro-CT imaging of dissolving shells, pore-scale modelling and dissolution experiments, with a particular interest in the role of organic matter. Passionate about the ocean, my aim is to get a better understanding of the importance of marine environments in mitigating climate change by dissolving calcium carbonates.

Before joining CEREGE I studied global change biology at the University of Antwerp, where I learned about different consequences of climate change and to help mitigate these effects.

Outside the lab you can find me somewhere hiking, chasing beautiful sunsets or enjoying a big cup of tea, usually accompanied with a homemade song and some jokes!
