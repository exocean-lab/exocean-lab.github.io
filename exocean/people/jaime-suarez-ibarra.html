Jaime Y. SUÁREZ-IBARRA

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Fellow at CEREGE, France

¡Hola!

I am a micropaleontologist and paleoceanographer specializing in late Neogene planktonic foraminifera. I work as a CNRS postdoctoral researcher in the Climate team at CEREGE, south of France. My research focuses on past climate changes and planktonic foraminifera ecological and morphological evolution.

I am curious about the processes that shaped the Earth climate system, languages, cultures and traveling.
